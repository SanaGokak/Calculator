THE SANAGKK PHILOSOPHY

Modesty is Not Absence of Style; It is The Pinnacle of It.

"I founded Modesty by SanaGKK because I was weary of seeing modest fashion treated as an afterthought in mainstream haute couture. Modest women deserve garments meticulously engineered with bespoke tailoring, non-see-through pure silks, and silhouettes that radiate effortless authority."

Every silhouette in our collection is developed between our studios in Dubai and London. We work exclusively with certified ethical textile mills that produce heavyweight, breathable Dubai Nida, French-woven Medina silks, and eco-certified organic linens.

Uncompromising Opacity

Every bolt of fabric is rigorously tested against studio backlighting to guarantee absolute zero transparency.

Harmonious Palette

Curated around Navy Blue, Warm Sand Beige, and Slate Gray for mix-and-match capsule longevity.

Bespoke Fit

Standard lengths from 52" to 60" plus complimentary atelier tailoring for a flawless drape.

Frequently Asked Questions

Insights into our sizing, fabric provenance, and worldwide bespoke delivery.

Abaya sizing corresponds to the total shoulder-to-hem length in inches. A client of 5'2" typically selects Size 52, 5'4" wears Size 54, 5'6" wears Size 56, and so forth. If you prefer to wear high heels, we recommend sizing up one length. You can also utilize our interactive Height Calculator above.

Founder Sana Khan designed this signature triad to facilitate timeless capsule wardrobing. Midnight Navy delivers sovereign authority, Warm Sand Beige exudes desert warmth and understated modesty, while Slate Gray adds sharp architectural neutrality.

We ship worldwide with DHL and FedEx Express. Orders over $150 USD receive complimentary express shipping. Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days, with tracking updates dispatched to your mobile and email. We offer a 30-day unworn return window with all tags and dustbags attached.

We recommend dry cleaning or delicate hand washing with cold water and mild detergent. When steaming, use a garment steamer on low-to-medium heat. Medina Silks should always be steamed on reverse to preserve the buttery lustrous finish.
